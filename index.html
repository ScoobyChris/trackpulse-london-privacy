TRACKPULSE LONDON

Privacy notice

Effective 4 September 2026

TrackPulse London does not require an account and does not operate an application server. The app does not include advertising or analytics SDKs and its operator does not collect, sell, or share personal data.

Network requests

The app connects directly to the Transport for London Unified API to obtain routes, arrivals, and service status. TfL receives the network information normally supplied with an internet request, such as your IP address. TfL handles that information under its own privacy terms.

Information stored on your device

Route data is cached for up to seven days. Spotter-mode preferences and tags are also saved locally. This information remains on your device and is not sent to the app operator. Clear the app's storage or uninstall it to remove this information.

Children and sensitive information

The app is a general-audience transport utility. It does not knowingly collect information from children or request sensitive personal data.

Changes and contact

Material changes will be reflected on this page and in a subsequent release. Privacy questions can be raised through the project's public issue tracker. Do not post personal or sensitive information there.
