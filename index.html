TRACK PULSE

Privacy notice

Effective 13 November 2026

Track Pulse does not require an account. The app does not include advertising or analytics SDKs, and its operator does not sell or share personal data.

Network requests

The app connects to transport-authority services and the Track Pulse live-data service to obtain routes, arrivals, and service status. These services receive the information normally supplied with an internet request, such as your IP address.

Nearest station

If you choose Near me, the app requests your approximate location while it is in use. The nearest station is calculated on your device. Your location is not stored, sent to the app operator, or used for advertising. You can continue to choose stations manually if you do not grant permission.

Information stored on your device

Route data is cached for up to seven days. Your selected city, tracked trains, saved stations, journeys, alerts and app preferences are also saved locally. This information remains on your device and is not sent to the app operator. Clear the app's storage or uninstall it to remove this information.

Journey notifications

If you create a journey alert, its station, calling-point and timing choices are stored on your device. Notification permission is optional and is requested only when you create an alert. You can remove an alert in the app or change notification permission in your device settings.

Track Pulse Plus purchases

Track Pulse Plus purchases and restorations are processed by Apple or Google through your store account. Track Pulse does not receive or store your payment-card details.

Children and sensitive information

The app is a general-audience transport utility. It does not knowingly collect information from children or request sensitive personal data.

Changes and contact

Material changes will be reflected on this page and in a subsequent release. Privacy questions can be raised through the project's public issue tracker. Do not post personal or sensitive information there.
